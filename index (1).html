For most people, Ryanair is just a flight. For me, it’s the reason I’ve seen so much of Europe.

I’d love to help build the thing that got me there.

Why this isn’t a generic application

During my exchange semester in Rotterdam I was a student on a budget with a long list of places to see. Ryanair made the list possible. I chose long layovers in far-off airports to save a little extra, because every euro saved meant one more city, one more conversation with a stranger, one more step outside my comfort zone.

Today I live in Luxembourg, far from my family in Portugal, and Ryanair is what keeps going home affordable. Low fares, for me, mean more time with the people I love.

I want to understand how a finance team makes that possible, and help keep it possible for the next student with a long list and a small budget.
